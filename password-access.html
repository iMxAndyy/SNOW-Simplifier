🔑 Turn on reset page detection

When you open the reset page for one of the accounts on your Password Expiry list, a small bar asks whether you reset that account's password. Answer Yes and today is recorded as the date it was last changed.

  • To do this, Edge has to let the SNOW Simplifier run on the websites you visit.
  • It only compares each page's address with your accounts' reset links. Nothing is read from the page, and passwords are never read or stored.
  • You can turn it off at any time on the Password Expiry page, which removes the access again.
